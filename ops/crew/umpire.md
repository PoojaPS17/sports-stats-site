Before calling anything a mismatch, apply these ESPN counting rules, because a literal sum does not match how ESPN's own headline totals are built:
    - ESPN's headline season totals count regular-season games only. Preseason, play-in games, an All-Star or Pro Bowl game and an NBA Cup final are excluded from the totals even when a fuller game log lists them.
    - A player who changed teams during the season has one ESPN row per team plus a combined "Totals" row; use the Totals row when it exists. It does not always exist, so a single per-team row can be the whole season for some players.
    - For NBA games played, ESPN counts any game where the player has a minutes entry, including a zero-minute entry, but not a bench row marked with a dash instead of a number.
    - For NFL games played, ESPN counts a game whenever the player is listed at all, even with no recorded stat line (common for linemen and special-teamers), so ESPN's games-played figure is often higher than a count built only from box-score rows that have a stat.
    - Treat a shortfall in games played as explained by these rules, and not a mismatch, when the per-game stat totals otherwise agree; only a real difference in the season point or stat totals is a finding.

## Severity

Stale heartbeats, a league whose feed has gone quiet while ESPN shows results, a tick age over 150 minutes, and a score mismatch against ESPN are critical. Duplicates, refetching, null scores, orphan rows and a missing or halved backup are high. Volume drops, backup age over 26 hours and a dead row ratio over 0.2 are medium. Standings sum mismatches, idle teams and unresolved F1 sessions are low. Disk free under 8 percent is critical; under 15 percent is high. Available memory under 10 percent, app memory doubled in seven days, app errors over 20 in a day, pending security updates or a required reboot held for more than seven days, NTP not synchronised, Oracle idle-load risk and a slow database statement over 500 ms mean time are medium. A failed systemd unit, a timer with no next run, app restarts up since yesterday, and app errors over 200 in a day are high. An origin certificate under 30 days is high. The leaguewide second-source score check (step 21) is critical, matching step 11. Standings differences, player-totals differences, stale fixtures, kickoff-time mismatches, empty pages and a league missing its coming week's schedule are high. A sequential-scan-heavy table, an unreadable journal, an unreadable certificate file and an unknown NTP reading are low. Mojibake and missing site-search results are medium.
